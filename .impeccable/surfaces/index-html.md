# Surface brief: index.html (the whole site)

Scope: one continuous page, Read mode. The visitor (a Copenhagen recruiter, hiring manager or DTU supervisor) came to understand who Kostis is, what he has built, what he does now, and how to reach him. Action: email or open a repository. Proof: the CV facts and the linked GitHub repositories. Constraint: nothing about Amass beyond role title and a public one-liner; no phone number; print must yield a clean CV PDF.

Unresolved: thesis title (not fixed); whether to add a downloadable PDF later (print stylesheet stands in for it).

## Direction contract

THESIS: A career read as a Copenhagen transit line diagram: one continuous line down the left margin with a station for every section and role, a "you are here" marker that follows the reader. It refuses the category default of a centred hero portrait over a grid of same-size project cards, and refuses the LaTeX-paper CV look.

OWN-WORLD: Near-white cool ground (#F7F8F7), ink #15181B, one committed signal red (#C8102E) that owns the line, the station dots, the masthead rule and the links; hairlines #C9CBC9 at 1px; tabular mono labels for dates and codes. Type: Overpass (highway-signage grotesk) at 400/600/800 for display and body, Overpass Mono for dates, codes and small labels; no italics. Components: station rows (dot on the line, mono date, title), ruled timetable tables for projects and skills, filled 0-radius red primary button, 1px outlined secondary. Strip all content and the red line with its dots and a ruled table still say "Copenhagen line diagram".

STORY: In the first viewport the reader learns the name, the one-line position (mathematician turned AI engineer, MSc Human-Centered AI at DTU, Copenhagen), what he does now, and how to contact him. Scrolling, the line carries them station by station through experience, projects, thesis, education, skills; each project proves itself with a repository link. They leave believing the facts are real and knowing how to get in touch.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left margin: the red line starts under the masthead with the first station dot. Masthead band: name set large (Overpass 800, about 64px desktop), under it the one-line position, then the contact row (email, GitHub, LinkedIn, Print CV) as mono labels. Right of the line, the reading column (about 68ch) opens with the profile paragraph and a "Now" block listing the two current roles and the planned thesis as the next three stations. A sticky top strip shows the current station name and the section list as the line's stations. The primary action (email) sits in the contact row of the masthead.

FORM: Copenhagen S-tog / Metro line diagram as the frame of a CV document. Position 3 on my ordered list of seven grounded candidates; the roll assigned index 3. Seed key e45b09d8 (mode read, scope direction). Raises from the hand: from ikeda-datamatics, hairline precision and tabular numerals throughout; from the cutting bench, state as a mark not a hue (the current role is a hollow ring, past roles filled, the future thesis a dashed continuation); from the design annual, one grotesque for display and caption with no italics; from the catalog sleeve, vast unprinted space between stations; from the provenance ribbon, known versus planned rendered solid versus dashed; from the alphabet storm, one motion event only (the marker travelling the line). Declined challengers: ikeda, cutting bench, catalog sleeve, alphabet storm. Competitive: design annual (clarity), provenance ribbon (identification).
